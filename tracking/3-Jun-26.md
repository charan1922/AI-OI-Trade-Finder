### Pivot Points 🔲 TODO
Pivot points are intraday support/resistance levels derived from the previous day's High, Low, and Close. The central pivot (P) is the average of HLC; levels above it (R1, R2, R3) act as resistance and levels below (S1, S2, S3) act as support. Price trading above P signals bullish bias, below P signals bearish bias. (U share - refer tf videos)

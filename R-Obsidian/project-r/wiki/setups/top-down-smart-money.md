# Top-Down Smart Money (the TF trader's method)

The TF trader's end-to-end approach, distilled from
[[2026-07-06-tf-video-method-notes|the user's video-analysis notes]]. This is
the method every trade page is read against.

## The funnel — never pick a stock in isolation

1. **Sector first ("Sector Scope").** Find the strongest/weakest sector on the
   day (heatmap green/red; e.g. a PSU-bank rally at 4.12× strength, an IT
   downturn at −9.17×). Trade with the sector tide.
2. **Economic logic.** Apply macro reasoning: IT/pharma as safe havens during
   Rupee depreciation (dollar earners); avoid what the macro is against.
3. **Stock via R-Factor.** Inside the chosen sector, use Intraday Boost /
   Breakout Beacon to find where institutions are aggressively building
   positions (examples from the notes: ABB at 10:15 AM ₹36k→+₹23k; ADANIGREEN
   960 PE +₹16k on aggressive-selling signals).

## Execution — option buying for momentum

- **Momentum over range.** Sideways markets erode premium (theta); only trade
  one-way moves.
- **Confirmation over greed.** Let the market settle 30–60 min after open; no
  "morning haste". Enter after the breakout / institutional confirmation, often
  after a small pullback (TATASTEEL puts example: waited for the morning
  downfall + pullback, ₹40k→+₹17k).
- **Avoid toxic volatility.** No lower-circuit stocks, no scam/news names
  (UPL, IDFCFIRSTB episodes) — the data is unreliable there.

## Risk & psychology — the most emphasized pillar

- **Small losses are good.** Losses capped at ~₹2.5k; if the trade reverses
  right after entry, tighten the stop to the cut point and exit (DIXON 11200 PE
  −₹2.5k, LAURUSLABS −₹2.5k examples). The 24-day winning streak came from tiny
  losses, not from avoiding losses.
- **Capital efficiency.** ₹25k–₹60k per trade, 1–4 lots, target ₹14k–₹23k per
  winner. Matches the user's own ₹50–60k budget.
- **Hedging use.** In bear markets, disciplined put buying doubles as a hedge
  for a bleeding investment portfolio (PERSISTENT 4600 PE example).
- **Mindset.** Confidence in the method (no hesitation on a clear signal),
  consistency over home runs, process over P&L.

# Entry Setups & Method — the TF trader (Pro Trader Aakash)

The single reference for how the TF trader trades: the top-down method AND his
four entry triggers, distilled from two sources —
[[2026-07-06-tf-video-method-notes|the method notes]] and
[[2026-07-06-entry-setups-video|the entry-setups video summary]]
(youtube CXE-RrP5_ys, 20:56). Every trade page is read against this.

## Importance ranking — entry comes LAST

![[entry-setups-04-05-ranking.png]]

1. **Momentum** — never trade sideways; of the ~200 F&O stocks, 5–6 move 4–6%
   on any day, and their options can pay 200–300% intraday.
2. **Risk management** — size to risk capacity.
3. **Exit** — know both exits (SL and target) *before* buying.
4. **Entry** — only then does the entry setup matter. An entry strategy alone
   does not make a profitable trader.

## The funnel — never pick a stock in isolation

1. **Market first.** Trade in the broader market's (Nifty's) direction.

   ![[entry-setups-09-26-nifty-direction.png]]

2. **Sector next ("Sector Scope").** Find the strongest/weakest sector on the
   day; trade with the sector tide. Macro logic applies (IT/pharma as safe
   havens on Rupee weakness — dollar earners).

   ![[entry-setups-10-19-sector-scope.png]]

3. **Stock via R-Factor.** Inside the chosen sector, Intraday Boost / Breakout
   Beacon show where institutions are aggressively building positions.

   ![[entry-setups-10-48-intraday-boost.png]]

## The four entry setups

![[entry-setups-06-36-four-setups.png]]

### 1 · Morning Base Entry (video 06:10)

![[entry-setups-07-18-morning-base.png]]

- **Logic:** for a stock to trend all day, big players must be driving it —
  their footprint shows early.
- **Rule (bullish):** the low of the **first 30 minutes must stay unbroken**.
  A held morning low = sellers lack strength, buyers in control. (Mirror for
  bearish: first-30-min high holds.)
- **Execution:** he rarely trades before 10:00 — by then there's ~45 min of
  data. Base intact → longs on pullbacks/breakouts with the trend behind you.
- Our ADANIPORTS PE pilot is the bearish mirror in action: entry 10:20 after
  the fall was established ([[2026-06-23-ADANIPORTS-1780-PE]]).

### 2 · Momentum Pullback Entry (video 11:08)

![[entry-setups-12-32-pivot-pullback.png]]

- **Tool:** Standard Pivot Points.
- **Rule:** Morning Base intact first, then wait for a natural pullback to a
  standard pivot level.
- **Execution:** never buy blindly at the pivot — wait for the test AND the
  bounce back in the trend's direction; enter on the favorable candle close.
- **SL:** just beyond the low of the entry candle (or the one before it) —
  tight stop, high reward.

### 3 · Flag Breakout Entry (video 15:21)

![[entry-setups-16-06-flag-godrejprop.png]]

- **Logic:** pole = institutional surge; flag = weak counter-move; breakout
  continues the surge.
- **Rule:** **no pole → no flag.** A random sideways box breakout doesn't
  qualify; the strong preceding directional move is mandatory.
- **Execution:** wait for a candle **close outside** the flag (rectangle or
  triangle); enter the breakout.
- **SL:** the base of the flag structure.
- Video demo above: Godrej Prop 5-min bearish flag.

### 4 · Sandwich Entry (trap setup, video 18:00)

![[entry-setups-17-58-sandwich-polycab.png]]

![[entry-setups-20-10-sandwich-note.png]]

- **Bullish "green sandwich":** in an uptrend, one red candle appears (baits
  short-sellers), then a green candle immediately traps it. Sellers stuck.
- **Bearish "red sandwich":** one green candle trapped between two strong reds
  in a downtrend. Buyers stuck.
- **Execution:** enter when price breaks past the sandwich — the trapped
  traders' stop-losses add fuel ("overflow" of momentum).
- **SL:** the extreme high/low of the sandwich structure.
- Video demo above: Polycab 5-min.

## Risk & psychology — the most emphasized pillar

- **Small losses are good.** Losses capped at ~₹2.5k; if the trade reverses
  right after entry, tighten the stop to the cut point and exit (DIXON 11200 PE
  −₹2.5k, LAURUSLABS −₹2.5k examples). The 24-day winning streak came from tiny
  losses, not from avoiding losses.
- **Avoid toxic volatility.** No lower-circuit stocks, no scam/news names
  (UPL, IDFCFIRSTB episodes) — the data is unreliable there.
- **Capital efficiency.** ₹25k–₹60k per trade, 1–4 lots, target ₹14k–₹23k per
  winner. Matches the user's own ₹50–60k budget.
- **Hedging use.** In bear markets, disciplined put buying doubles as a hedge
  for a bleeding investment portfolio (PERSISTENT 4600 PE example).
- **Patience over frequency (video 20:02).** Don't force a trade; a missed
  entry is replaced by the next one. Deploy only when momentum + risk +
  planned exit are all aligned.

2. **Risk Management:** You must know your risk capacity and position size appropriately.
3. **Exit Strategy:** Before clicking "buy," you must already know exactly where you will exit if the trade goes against you, or where you will take profits.

### The 4 "Secret" Entry Setups

Once the prerequisites are met, Aakash relies on four specific technical setups to trigger his entries.

#### 1. Morning Base Entry Setup [[06:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=370)]

This is a foundational setup used to identify if institutional "smart money" is in control of a stock.

* **The Logic:** For a stock to trend strongly all day, big players must be driving the price. You can spot this footprint early in the morning.
* **The Rule:** If you are looking to buy (bullish), **the low established in the first 30 minutes of the trading day must remain unbroken.** If that 30-minute low holds, it proves sellers lack the strength to push the price down, and buyers are in full control [[07:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=422)].
* **Execution:** Aakash rarely trades before 10:00 AM. By 10:00 AM, he has 45 minutes of chart data. If the morning base is fully intact, he knows the trend is reliable, and he will look for long entries on pullbacks or breakouts, knowing the broader momentum is safely behind him.

#### 2. Momentum Pullback Entry [[11:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=668)]

This is a specific, rule-based setup designed to remove the emotion and guesswork from finding a pullback entry.

* **The Tool:** He uses the **Standard Pivot Points** indicator.
* **The Rule:** First, confirm that the Morning Base (Setup 1) is intact. Once established, wait for the stock's price to naturally pull back (retrace) toward a standard pivot point level [[12:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=746)].
* **Execution:** Do not buy blindly at the pivot. Wait for the price to test the pivot line and bounce back in the direction of the main trend. Enter when the candle closes favorably after bouncing off the pivot.
* **Risk Management:** Place your stop-loss just below the low of the entry candle or the preceding candle. This offers a very tight stop-loss with high reward potential.

#### 3. Flag Breakout Pattern [[15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=921)]

While "breakout trading" is common, Aakash uses a strict filter to ensure he doesn't get caught in fake-outs.

* **The Logic:** A valid flag pattern represents a massive surge of institutional buying (the Pole), followed by a weak attempt by sellers to drop the price (the Flag). Because the sellers are weak, the price eventually breaks out to continue its original massive surge.
* **The Rule:** **A Flag is completely invalid if it does not have a strong "Pole."** Aakash warns against trading random sideways box breakouts. The preceding strong directional move (the pole) is mandatory because it proves big money is involved [[16:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=999)].
* **Execution:** Wait for a clear candle close *outside* the flag structure (which can be rectangular or triangular). Enter on the breakout and place your stop-loss at the base (bottom) of the flag structure.

#### 4. The Sandwich Entry (Trap Setup) [[18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=1080)]

This setup relies on market psychology and forces trapped retail traders to fuel your profits when they panic and close their losing positions.

* **The Bullish "Green Sandwich":** Look for a strong uptrend. Suddenly, a single Red candle forms, tricking retail bears into short-selling. Immediately after, another Green candle forms, completely trapping the red candle in the middle. The sellers are now trapped [[19:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=1162)].
* **The Bearish "Red Sandwich":** A single Green candle gets trapped completely between two strong Red candles during a downtrend. Buyers are now trapped.
* **Execution:** Once the price breaks out past the sandwich formation, the trapped traders will panic and hit their stop-losses. This sudden rush of exit orders creates an "overflow" of momentum. You enter on this breakout, placing your stop-loss at the extreme high or low of the sandwich structure.

### Final Takeaways

Aakash concludes by emphasizing that all four setups require immense patience [[20:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CXE-RrP5_ys&t=1202)]. Do not force trades. If you miss one entry, the market will eventually present another. Ensure you are always trading in the direction of the broader market (Nifty) and only deploy capital when momentum, risk parameters, and your planned exit are strictly aligned.

## How it grows

1. Drop a source into `raw/` — video summaries and articles into
   `raw/articles/`, screenshots into `raw/assets/`, session notes into
   `raw/sessions/`, scorecards into `raw/scorecards/`.
2. Ask the LLM to **ingest** it. The format is agreed with you first; raw
   sources stay verbatim and immutable; derived understanding goes into the
   consolidated wiki pages (few docs, not many).
3. Ask questions — good answers get filed back so exploration compounds.
4. Occasionally ask for a **lint**: contradictions, stale claims, gaps.

Conventions live in [[CLAUDE|the schema]]; human-facing setup notes in the
README. Trader context: Indian F&O, ₹50–60k capital, near-ATM option buying
(CE bullish / PE bearish), 09:40–11:00 IST morning window.
